Enable Banking

Online Bank Statements Synchronization

Automatically sync your bank statements with Enable Banking API connector. Enable Banking supports over 2500 banks in 28 countries in Europe. If you link your own accounts on Enable Banking website, you can use their API FOR FREE.
See the full list of supported banks here: https://enablebanking.com/open-banking-apis

Clear overview

Fetch transactions manually, have a clear overview of your bank connection status and access the Enable Banking configuration right from the journal dashboard.

Simple setup

Just insert your Application ID and RSA Private Key, then click 'Connect' to effortlessly establish the connection. It's a quick and easy process, so you can get your bank sync running smoothly in no time.

Setup & Requirements

Ensure you have pyjwt pip package installed before you install the module.

  1. Make sure that the bank that is selected on your Journal has correct name and country as listed here: https://enablebanking.com/open-banking-apis
  2. Go to your Journal Dashboard and click Connect on the journal you wish to connect with Enable Banking
  3. Copy the Redirect URL that is showed in the Enable Banking Connection Wizard
  4. Go to https://enablebanking.com/cp/applications (create your account if you don't have one)
  5. On the Enable Banking control panel, create a new production environment (follow the instructions on the page)
  6. Link your account(s) you wish to connect with Odoo
  7. Go back to the Enable Banking connection wizard in your Odoo and fill in Application ID and RSA Key
  8. Click Connect and follow the instructions
  9. Everything is ready!
